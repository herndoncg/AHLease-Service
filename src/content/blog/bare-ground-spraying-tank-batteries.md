Grass and weeds inside a firewall or around a wellhead are more than an eyesore. They are fuel for a fire, they hide leaks and corrosion, and they make a location look neglected on an inspection or a sale.

## What bare ground means

A bare-ground treatment keeps all vegetation off a set area for most of a growing season. It is different from the selective spraying used on rights-of-way, which takes out brush and broadleaf weeds and leaves the grass that holds the soil.

## When to spray

In East Texas the growing season runs roughly March into October. The best results come from:

- **An early spring application,** before weeds are up, so the product is in place when growth starts.
- **A follow-up later in the season,** to catch anything that comes through. Heavy rain, soil type and the weeds on site all affect how long a treatment holds.

## Where it goes

Tank batteries and inside firewalls, around wellheads and pumping units, along location fence lines, and around separators, meter runs and risers.

## What to watch for

What sits next to the treated area matters. Pasture, crops, ponds and creeks next to a location change which product is right and how it is applied. That is a conversation to have before the first application, not after.
